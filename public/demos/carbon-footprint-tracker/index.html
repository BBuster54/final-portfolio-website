Methodology

  • Transportation: emission rate × miles/week × 4.33 (weeks/month)
  • Home energy: kWh/month × 0.4 kg CO₂/kWh (U.S. average grid estimate)
  • Diet: category-based daily estimate × 30 days

All emission factors are illustrative estimates for demonstration purposes, not precise scientific measurements.
